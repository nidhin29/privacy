Privacy Policy for Spendy

Last Updated: June 21, 2026 3 min read

Permanent retirement notice

Notice dated: 18 November 2026

Spendy has been permanently discontinued. The final retirement update keeps existing financial records available to view and export as PDF or CSV. AI, new transaction entry, automatic SMS tracking and reminders are disabled.

  • The update removes build 8's downloaded chat model and its app-owned temporary copies. Your saved financial records are not automatically deleted.
  • Existing local records are protected through a verified encryption migration. If migration cannot be completed safely, the app preserves existing files and offers a retry; successful migration cannot be guaranteed on every device.
  • PDF and CSV exports are not encrypted and exclude processed SMS logs and pending raw SMS.
  • You can explicitly delete saved financial data in the app or uninstall when finished. Uninstalling removes the app's private local data. Previously exported files, copies shared elsewhere and existing Google Drive backups are not automatically deleted.

These retirement changes take effect only after the update is installed and opened. Older installations retain their earlier behavior. The remaining sections below describe earlier versions; they do not mean retired features remain available.

Spendy stores financial records in private app storage on your device. SMS parsing and Qwen inference run locally. Optional Google Drive backups and exports send data to destinations you choose. 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Spendy ("we," "our," or "the Application"), developed by Proddec, processes, stores, and protects your information. By using Spendy, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

What is Offline-First? It means the core database, ML classification engines, and storage reside completely inside your phone's sandbox storage. No user accounts are tracked on our servers.

2. Types of Data Processed

Financial records are stored in the application's private device storage. If you choose Drive backup or export, copies leave that storage. Voice input is handled by the device's speech-recognition service, which may use online processing.

Financial Transactions

Records of your income, expenses, accounts, categories, and savings goals mapped and classified locally.

SMS Transaction Logs

Parsed text metadata (such as amount, transaction type, and merchant name) extracted from incoming SMS bank alerts.

Account Details

Wallet names, custom bank names, and the last 4 digits of your account/card suffix to help group alerts.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To enable intelligent tracking and automated workflows, Spendy requires specific Android system permissions. How these permissions are used is detailed below, including the device speech-recognition service:

SMS Permissions

RECEIVE_SMS & READ_SMS
Local-Only Processing

Usage: Used to automatically intercept and parse incoming bank transaction alerts in real time.

Privacy Guard: Incoming SMS content is filtered locally on your device. Only alerts matching specific financial patterns (like debited/credited alerts) are analyzed. The SMS text is parsed by our local regular expression engines and on-device AI algorithms. SMS parsing runs locally. Saved SMS-derived records may be included in backups or exports you choose to create.

Camera Access & OCR Scanning

CAMERA
Local-Only Processing

Usage: Used to capture photos of physical store purchase receipts to scan, extract items, and create structured itemized entries.

Privacy Guard: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on-device using local ML Kit models. Receipt images may also be retained in app storage and included in backups where supported. Spendy does not upload images to a cloud OCR service.

Microphone Access & Speech-to-Text

RECORD_AUDIO
Device Speech Service

Usage: Used to record voice logs of manual expenses to let you log transactions hands-free.

Privacy Guard: Speech recognition uses the service configured on your device. Depending on the provider, installed language support and settings, audio may be sent to that provider's servers under its privacy policy. Spendy does not guarantee offline recognition. The resulting transcript is processed by the app's local model to prepare transaction details.

Biometric Authentication

USE_BIOMETRIC
Local-Only Processing

Usage: Used to unlock your personal App Vault and Settings lock to prevent unauthorized physical access to your financial data.

Privacy Guard: Biometric verification is managed by your Android operating system. Spendy never gains access to, processes, or stores your biometric credentials (fingerprints or face models).

Local Storage (Read/Write)

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Local-Only Processing

Usage: Used to export transaction logs as CSV or PDF documents to your device storage, and to save backup files locally.

Privacy Guard: The app only accesses folder paths that you explicitly choose. It does not scan your personal media library or files.

4. On-Device AI Processing (Qwen LLM)

Spendy utilizes local Large Language Model (LLM) inference (Qwen) running natively on your device's CPU/GPU. The semantic classification of your transaction descriptions into budget categories happens completely offline, ensuring your reading and spending habits are never exposed to commercial AI servers.

Model Credit & Disclaimer: The local AI capabilities are powered by open-source model weights (specifically the Qwen model series developed by Alibaba Cloud). 5. Optional Cloud Backup (Google Drive Integration)

You may voluntarily connect your Google Account to back up your database to your personal Google Drive storage space:

AES-256

Backup Encryption

Cloud backup encryption is separate from the live database. Build 8's local SQLite database does not have app-level encryption. Do not assume that receipt files, temporary files, CSV/PDF reports or every older backup format are encrypted. Backup protection depends on the app version, credentials and device security.

Direct Sync

Direct Synchronization

Data is transferred directly from your device to your personal Google Drive storage space. We do not host an intermediate backup server. The app uses Google authorization to access your Drive backup; Google processes that transfer under its own policies.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, it is retained until you choose to delete it. You can instantly wipe your entire local SQLite database, custom categories, budgets, and keys by using the "Wipe All Data" button inside the settings page.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendy is suitable for all age groups, including children (IARC 3+). Spendy requires no app registration and keeps financial records in private device storage. The voice-service, export and optional Drive-backup processing described above also applies to younger users. Parents or guardians can inspect, manage, or delete this local database at any time by using the "Wipe All Data" button in the app settings or by clearing the application storage in device settings.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any updates directly in the repository and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We recommend visiting this page occasionally to check for updates.
